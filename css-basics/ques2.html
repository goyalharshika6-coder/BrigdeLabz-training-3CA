Welcome to Paris

Paris is one of the world's most famous cities. It is known for its beautiful architecture, art, fashion, food and historical landmarks.

History

Paris has a long and fascinating history. The city developed around the River Seine and became an important center of culture, politics and education.

Today, millions of visitors travel to Paris every year to experience its historical buildings and famous landmarks.

Culture

Paris is famous for its art, museums, cuisine and fashion. The city has influenced artists, writers, designers and filmmakers around the world.
