The Importance of Good Typography

Why Typography Matters

Good typography makes a webpage easier to read and more attractive. Choosing the right font family, size, and weight helps create a clear visual structure. A well-designed page should have enough spacing between lines and letters so that the reader can easily understand the content.

Readable Text

Different font families can create different feelings and styles. Serif fonts such as Georgia look traditional, while sans-serif fonts such as Arial provide a clean appearance.

Using proper line-height and letter-spacing improves readability and makes the page visually balanced.

Text can also be emphasized using strong text and italic text.

Conclusion

Good typography makes web content attractive, clear and easy to read.
